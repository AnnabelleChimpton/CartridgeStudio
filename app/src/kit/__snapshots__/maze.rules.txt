When Cat touches Dust ghost: take 1 from Lives, send every Cat back to start, protect every Cat for 1.5 seconds and play Falling tone.
When Cat touches Bat: take 1 from Lives, send every Cat back to start, protect every Cat for 1.5 seconds and play Falling tone.
When the number of Yarn balls is 0: play Chime and win the game.
When Lives is 0: lose the game.
