When Cat touches Key, remove the Key, add 1 to Keys, add 50 points and play Rising tone.
When Cat touches Pantry door, only if Keys is at least 1, remove the Pantry door, take 1 from Keys, add 100 points and play Chime.
When Cat touches Pantry door, only if Keys is 0, send the Cat back to start and play Falling tone.
When Cat touches Cat treat, remove the Cat treat, add 20 points and play Blip.
When Cat touches Robot vacuum, take 1 from Lives, send every Cat back to start, protect every Cat for 1.5 seconds and play Crash.
When a Cat is on spikes, take 1 from Lives, send every Cat back to start, protect every Cat for 1.5 seconds and play Falling tone.
When Cat touches Sardines, add 500 points, play Chime and win the game.
When Lives is 0, lose the game.
